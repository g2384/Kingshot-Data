Alliance Brawl Guide

Stay in your original alliance normally. Join the small alliance before the Day 1 reset to secure a top-20 rank, then join it again before Day 7 at 12:00 UTC to qualify for final rewards.

Daily checklist

Every day

  • Escort trucks for 10,000 points each
  • Raid trucks for 10,000 points each
  • Spend gems and top up

Day 0: Join + qualify

  • Before the Day 1 reset, join the small alliance
  • Ensure the alliance ranks in the top 20

Day 1: Gather + save Intel

  • Gather resources
  • Save Intel for Day 2

Day 2: Intel + shards

  • Finish saved Intel: 3,000 each

Day 3: Gather + pets

  • Gather resources
  • Save Intel for Day 4

Day 4: Gear + troops

  • Train troops
  • Finish saved Intel: 3,000 each

Day 5: Beasts

  • Defeat beasts: 4,000-6,000 each
  • Hunt Terrors in rallies: 15,000 each

Day 6: Final push

  • Defeat beasts and hunt Terrors
  • Use remaining upgrade materials
  • Check 300K personal points

Day 7: Join + claim

  • Join the small alliance before 12:00 UTC
  • Confirm alliance points exceed 20M
  • Check 300K personal points
  • Claim final rewards
