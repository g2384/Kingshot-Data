Alliance Brawl Small Alliance Guide

  • Find 5-10 players before the event starts.
  • Agree on the order upfront: 1st, 2nd, 3rd-5th, and 6th-10th.
  • 3rd-5th get the same rewards, and 6th-10th get the same rewards.
  • Follow this order by Day 7 at 12:00 UTC. It does not matter on Day 1 or Day 2.
  • Stay in your original alliance, but do all scoring actions in the small alliance.
  • Join the small alliance before the Day 1 reset for a top-20 rank, then rejoin before Day 7 at 12:00 UTC for final rewards.

Daily checklist

Every day

  • Escort trucks for 10,000 points each
  • Raid trucks for 10,000 points each
  • Spend gems and top up

Day 0 (SUN): Join + qualify

  • Before the Day 1 reset, join the small alliance
  • Ensure the alliance ranks in the top 20

Day 1 (MON): Gather + save Intel

  • Send troops to gather in the original alliance; recall them in the small alliance later
  • Save 16UTC Intel Missions

Day 2 (TUE): Intel

  • Finish Intel Missions

Day 3 (WED): Gather + save Intel

  • Send troops to gather in the original alliance; recall them in the small alliance later
  • Save 16UTC Intel Missions

Day 4 (THU): Intel + troops

  • Train troops
  • Finish Intel Missions

Day 5 (FRI): Beasts

  • Defeat beasts and hunt Terrors

Day 6 (SAT): Beasts

  • Defeat beasts and hunt Terrors

Day 7 (SUN): Join + claim

  • Join the small alliance before 12:00 UTC
  • Confirm alliance points exceed 20M
  • Confirm personal points exceed 300K
  • Claim final rewards
